Child Safety Standards

Our Commitment

Hangr has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to providing a safe environment for all users and take active measures to prevent, detect, and respond to any content or behavior that endangers minors.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on Hangr:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or abuses minors
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to exploit children
  • Sharing, uploading, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Any communication intended to facilitate harm to a minor

Enforcement

Violations of this policy result in immediate account termination and reporting to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant law enforcement authorities, as required by law.

Reporting

If you encounter any content or behavior that violates these standards, please report it immediately by contacting us at:

Age Requirements

Hangr is intended for users aged 18 and older. Users who are found to be under the minimum age are removed from the platform.
