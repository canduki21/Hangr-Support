Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hangr ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Location data: your approximate location to show nearby events. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Usage data: events you create, swipes, and messages within the app.
  • Device information: push notification token to send you relevant alerts.

How We Use Your Information

  • To show you events near your location.
  • To connect you with other users interested in the same events.
  • To send push notifications about event requests and nearby hangouts.
  • To improve the app experience.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your information is only shared to operate the app (e.g., Supabase for database and authentication). We do not share your data with advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time through your device settings.
  • Request a copy of your data.

Children's Privacy

Hangr is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
