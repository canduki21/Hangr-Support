About hangear

hangear helps you discover events happening near you and find people to enjoy them with. Whether you're into live music, sports, food festivals, or just looking for something fun to do — hangear connects you with your next plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find events near me?
Open the app and browse events by category. hangear uses your location to show what's happening nearby.


How do I connect with other people?
Tap on an event to see who else is interested and start a conversation.


Is hangear free?
Yes, hangear is free to download and use.
