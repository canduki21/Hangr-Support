Features

Everything you need
to make plans happen

From discovering hangouts to hosting your own, Hangear covers the full social experience.

🃏
Swipe-based Discovery

Browse a live stack of events near you. Swipe right when you're down, swipe up to save for later, or skip — it's that simple. No endless scrolling, no noise.

🃏
🎉
Create & Host Hangouts

Set up your own event in minutes. Pick a category, location, time, and how many people you want. Approve guests manually or let everyone in automatically.

💬
Group Chats Included

Every approved event gets its own group chat — no setup needed. Coordinate plans, share your location, and get the vibe going before you even arrive.

👥
Friends & Direct Messages

Add friends, send requests, and message anyone directly. Build your social circle event by event.

⭐
Reviews & Reputation

After each hangout, leave a star rating and comment. Build a trusted reputation so great hosts and guests stand out in the community.

🎨
Rich Profiles

Showcase who you are with up to 6 photos, 3+ interests from 100+ options, your occupation, Instagram, and more. Hosts and guests always know who they're meeting.

🎨
How it works

From zero to hangout
in three steps

No complicated setup. Just open the app, find your vibe, and go.

1
Set up your profile

Add your photos, pick your interests, and set your search radius. Takes under 5 minutes.

2
Discover or create

Swipe through nearby events or tap Create to host your own hangout with full control over who joins.

3
Connect & show up

Chat with your group, coordinate the details, and enjoy the hangout. Leave a review when it's done.

Safety

Built for trust

Hangear is designed so you always know who you're meeting, and bad actors don't last.

🚩
Easy Reporting

Report any user or event in seconds with a clear reason. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours.

🛡
Zero Tolerance

Harassment, inappropriate content, and spam result in permanent bans — no appeals.

✅
Host Control

Hosts can manually approve every join request and revoke access at any time before the event.

🔒
Your Data is Yours

We collect only what's needed to run the service. We never sell your data to third parties.
